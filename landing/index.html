Three steps. No formulas.

You never write a formula, pick a chart type or learn a menu. You open the file and say what you want to know.

  1. 1

    Open your file

    Tap a CSV or Excel file in WhatsApp or Files and choose LUMIQ, or open it from the app. LUMIQ reads every column and, when a date could mean two things, it asks you instead of guessing.

  2. 2

    Ask in your own words

    "Which item sold the most?" "Total by month?" Type it the way you would say it. The sum, the average, the grouping are worked out on your phone from all of your rows.

  3. 3

    Trust it, then share it

    Every number in the answer is held against the calculation and marked when it matches. Play the Story of your file to see what moved and what stands out, and send any card as a picture.

It reads your file the way you wrote it.

Questions people ask.

Something else? Send it below.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything on your phone is free. Ask gives you 20 free questions a day.

Why an APK and not the Play Store?

LUMIQ is in early testing. The APK is signed with LUMIQ's own key, so every later version installs over this one and keeps your files.

How do I install it?

Download the APK on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps. If an older test version is on the phone, uninstall it first.

Which files can it open?

CSV files up to 30 MB and Excel files (.xlsx, .xls) up to 10 MB, up to 200,000 rows. Password-locked workbooks need the password removed first.

Does it need the internet?

Only for Ask. Opening files, Insights, the Story, File check, What if and Report all work offline.

Can I trust the numbers?

Every figure is calculated on your phone from all of your rows. In Ask, each number in the answer is compared with that calculation and marked when it matches; a number tied to a month or an item must match that row, not just any value.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. The whole app is in English and Hindi, and it understands lakh and crore.
